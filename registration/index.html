Registration

1. Registration Fee

Registration Fee

6,800 CNY

or 1,000 USD

The registration fee is the same for onsite and online presentation. The fee covers one paper accepted by MiTA 2027. Conference registration is now open. The registration and payment deadline is December 31, 2026. Authors of accepted papers are strongly encouraged to complete their registration and payment as early as possible.

Payment can be made either by bank transfer or by scanning the payment QR code. You may choose the option that suits you best.

3. Registration and Refund Policy

Registration Policy

  • An accepted paper will be published in the proceedings ONLY if at least one author registers for the paper.
  • MiTA 2027 provides both onsite and online presentation, and the registration fee is the same for both formats.
  • Authors can choose their preferred format when completing the registration form / payment.

Refund Policy

  • There will be no refunds to the authors.
For those unable to attend in person due to visa issues, online participation and presentation are available.
